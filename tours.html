Primate Special

Kibale Chimpanzee Tracking

📅 2 Days 👥 Max 8 pax 🌳 Kibale NP 💰 From $380/person

Kibale Forest harbours the world's highest concentration of primate species — 13 in total. The star attraction is the chimpanzee, genetically our closest relative sharing 98.7% of our DNA. Track them through dense tropical forest with expert primatologist guides.

  • ✔1 night at Kibale Forest Camp
  • ✔Crater lakes scenic drive (optional)
  • ✔All meals and transport from Fort Portal
