FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your Uganda safari.

When is the best time to visit Uganda for gorilla trekking?

Gorilla trekking is possible year-round. The driest months — June, July, August and December–February — make for easier trekking conditions and clearer forest trails. The "shoulder" seasons of March–May and September–November can be rewarding for keen adventurers, with lush green landscapes and fewer trekkers.

Do I need a visa to enter Uganda?

Most nationalities require a visa. Uganda operates an e-visa system at visas.immigration.go.ug. The single-entry tourist e-visa costs $50 USD. OTB PRO SAFARIS can guide you through the process at no extra charge. East African Community citizens (Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, etc.) enjoy visa-free entry.

How fit do I need to be for gorilla trekking?

Gorilla treks can last anywhere from 30 minutes to 8 hours depending on where the gorilla family has moved. The forest terrain can be steep and muddy. A moderate level of fitness is recommended. Walking sticks are provided, and porters are available for hire at the starting point — supporting local employment while easing your journey.

What vaccinations do I need for Uganda?

A Yellow Fever vaccination certificate is mandatory for entry into Uganda. We recommend consulting your travel health clinic 4–6 weeks before departure. Common recommendations include hepatitis A & B, typhoid, meningitis, and rabies. Anti-malarial prophylaxis is also strongly advised.

What currency is used and can I pay by card?

Uganda uses the Ugandan Shilling (UGX). US Dollars are widely accepted at lodges and for major payments. OTB PRO SAFARIS accepts bank transfer (USD/EUR/GBP), PayPal and credit card (Visa/Mastercard, 3% processing fee applies). A 30% deposit confirms your booking, with the balance due 30 days before arrival.

Is it safe to travel to Uganda?

Uganda is generally a very safe destination for tourists. The national parks and tourism circuits are well-patrolled and stable. Kampala is a vibrant, relatively safe capital — standard urban travel precautions apply. Our guides are trained in first aid and emergency response, and we maintain 24/7 on-ground support.
