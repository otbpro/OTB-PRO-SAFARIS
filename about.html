Our Story

From Kampala With Love, to the World

OTB PRO SAFARIS was born from a simple belief: Uganda is one of the most extraordinary places on earth, and more people deserve to experience it. Our founder, Charles Otim Buga, grew up in Gulu, spent his twenties guiding rangers through Murchison Falls, and eventually founded OTB PRO SAFARIS to share his country's magic with travellers from every corner of the globe.

Over 15 years and 1,200+ satisfied guests later, we remain a proudly Ugandan company. Every member of our team was born, raised and educated in Uganda. We don't outsource your experience to regional conglomerates — we craft every itinerary by hand, with genuine care and deep local knowledge.

We are licensed by the Uganda Tourism Board, accredited by the Association of Uganda Tour Operators (AUTO), and committed members of the Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) community outreach programme.

Conservation & Community

Travel That Gives Back

Safari tourism is the single most powerful tool for wildlife conservation in Uganda. Every time a traveller pays for a gorilla permit, that money funds ranger patrols that protect the forest from poaching and encroachment. OTB PRO SAFARIS takes this responsibility seriously.

Our Community Safari Fund allocates 10% of all tour revenues to three programmes: bursaries for children in Bwindi communities, micro-loans for women's craft cooperatives, and scholarships for ranger children attending secondary school in Kabale.

We use exclusively 4×4 diesel-electric hybrid vehicles where available, we enforce strict low-impact trekking protocols, and we refuse to take bookings for any activity that exploits wildlife for entertainment.

🦍 Gorilla Conservation

Funds anti-poaching patrols in Bwindi, in partnership with UWA.

📚 Education Fund

School bursaries for 45 children in Buhoma and Nkuringo villages.

🌱 Reforestation

1 tree planted per guest in buffer zones around Bwindi Forest.

Craft market partnerships ensuring fair trade for 120 artisans.
